  "border_sides": "all-4" | "bottom-only" | "left-only" | "top-only" | "none",
  "stroke_style": "solid" | "dashed" | "dotted" | "none",
  "stroke_width": "0px" | "1px" | "2px" | "4px" | "8px",
  "corner_radius": "sharp" | "subtle" | "medium" | "large" | "pill",

  "elevation": "none" | "subtle-drop" | "floating-drop" | "ring-only" | "stroke+shadow"
}

Definitions (Tailwind equivalents in parentheses):
- has_border: true if the card has an explicit border stroke on any edge, false otherwise.
- border_sides: which edges carry a stroke (border / border-b / border-l / border-t). If has_border is false, this is "none" (border-0).
- stroke_style: line pattern (border-solid / border-dashed / border-dotted). If has_border is false, this is "none".
- stroke_width: stroke thickness: 0px (border-0, none), 1px (border, hairline), 2px (border-2, medium), 4px (border-4, thick), 8px (border-8, heavy).
- corner_radius: corner curvature: sharp 0px (rounded-none), subtle 3px (near rounded-sm), medium 8px (rounded-lg), large 18px (near rounded-2xl), pill 9999px circular/capsule (rounded-full).
- corner_uniformity: all-corners (equal curvature on all 4 corners), top-only (only top corners rounded, bottom sharp; modal sheet), asymmetric (different radii; accent corner).
- elevation: box-shadow appearance: none (flat), subtle-drop (shadow), floating-drop (shadow-lg), ring-only (1px neutral outline near ring-1, without stroke), stroke+shadow (border plus shadow-md).

Card and canvas colors vary by theme; a visible boundary may come from a stroke, a shadow, or a tonal step.
